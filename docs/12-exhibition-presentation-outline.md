# Exhibition Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title
AI-Powered Cash Flow Forecasting and Decision Support System for SMEs

## Slide 2: Problem
SMEs often struggle to forecast cash flow and manage financial risk efficiently.

## Slide 3: Objective
To predict cash flow, explain predictions, simulate scenarios, and provide recommendations.

## Slide 4: System Flow
Business Data → Data Cleaning → Regression → SHAP → What-If → Monte Carlo → Recommendation → Dashboard

## Slide 5: Methodology
Explain the data pipeline and model training process.

## Slide 6: Model and Metrics
Multiple Linear Regression with MAE, RMSE, and R².

## Slide 7: Explainability
Use SHAP to show which features influence the prediction.

## Slide 8: What-If Analysis
Show how sales or expenses changes affect forecast output.

## Slide 9: Risk Simulation
Present Monte Carlo uncertainty range and risk threshold.

## Slide 10: Recommendation Engine
Show a rule-based suggestion based on prediction and threshold.

## Slide 11: Dashboard Demo
Display the Streamlit interface and highlight usage.

## Slide 12: Conclusion and Future Scope
Summarize the project value and possible future expansions.
