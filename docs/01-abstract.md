# Abstract

Cash flow management is one of the most critical aspects of business survival, especially for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). Many businesses struggle to predict financial risk because they lack a clear understanding of how sales, expenses, receivables, payables, and cash movement affect future cash position. This project presents an AI-powered cash flow forecasting and decision support system designed to help SMEs understand future cash-flow conditions and make better financial decisions.

The system uses historical business data, cleans and prepares it, and applies Multiple Linear Regression to forecast future cash flow. It further integrates SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to explain the contribution of each feature to the prediction, What-If analysis to simulate business change scenarios, Monte Carlo simulation to estimate uncertainty and risk range, and a rule-based recommendation engine to convert analytical results into simple and actionable financial advice.

The final output is a Streamlit-based dashboard that makes the complete workflow accessible to business users. It allows users to upload datasets, view financial trends, understand model explanations, simulate scenario changes, assess risk levels, and receive recommendations. The project is intentionally designed to be simple, explainable, and academically suitable for a college exhibition while still demonstrating real AI/ML decision-support functionality.

Keywords: Cash Flow Forecasting, Machine Learning, Multiple Linear Regression, SHAP, Monte Carlo Simulation, What-If Analysis, Streamlit, Decision Support System
