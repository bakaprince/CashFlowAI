# Literature Survey

## 1. Cash Flow Forecasting in SMEs
Cash flow forecasting is essential for financial planning, working-capital management, and business sustainability. SMEs often face difficulties because their operations are sensitive to fluctuations in sales, payment delays, and sudden expenses. Accurate cash flow forecasting helps organizations manage liquidity and reduce financial risk.

## 2. Traditional Forecasting Techniques
Traditional forecasting methods often rely on simple financial rules or spreadsheet-based calculations. These methods are straightforward but often limited in their ability to learn patterns from historical data. A regression-based approach offers a better balance between explainability and accuracy, especially for academic prototypes.

## 3. Machine Learning in Financial Forecasting
Machine learning methods are increasingly used in forecasting because they can learn relationships between variables like sales, operating costs, receivables, and cash position. Linear regression is often preferred for educational and prototype-level projects because it is easy to interpret and explain.

## 4. Explainable AI for Decision Support
The use of explainable AI is important in financial analysis because business users need to understand the reasons behind model predictions. SHAP provides a fair and interpretable way to explain the contribution of each feature to a model output, making the system more transparent.

## 5. Risk and Scenario Analysis
Decision support systems are more useful when users can test different business conditions. What-If analysis allows users to assess the effect of hypothetical changes, while Monte Carlo simulation helps present the range of possible outcomes under uncertainty.

## 6. Need for Simple and Explainable Systems
Although advanced deep learning models and LLM-based systems exist, they are often too complex for a student-level academic prototype. This project focuses on interpretability and feasibility. The use of Multiple Linear Regression, SHAP, and rule-based logic makes the system understandable to both students and evaluators.

## Conclusion
The literature indicates that financial forecasting and decision support benefit from a combination of statistical modeling, explainability, and scenario analysis. This project uses a simplified but effective system to demonstrate these capabilities in a college exhibition setting.
