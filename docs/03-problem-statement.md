# Problem Statement

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) often face financial uncertainty because they do not have a clear understanding of their future cash position. Cash flow problems may arise even when a business appears profitable, because income and expense timing are different. Without proper forecasting and decision support, businesses may fail to control expenses, delay payments, or miss investment opportunities.

Most SMEs still rely on manual spreadsheets or basic financial review, which is time-consuming and often does not explain the logic behind decisions. There is a need for a simple decision-support system that can:

- predict future cash flow from business data,
- explain which variables influence the prediction,
- estimate the impact of changes in business conditions,
- identify financial risk under uncertainty, and
- provide user-friendly recommendations.

This project addresses this problem by developing an AI-based cash-flow forecasting and decision support system that is both practical and easy to understand.
